OrangeBubbles Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

The short version: OrangeBubbles has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, and no tracking. We do not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal data — because the app is built so that we never receive any.

What we collect

Nothing. OrangeBubbles operates no backend. There is no sign-up, no account, no telemetry, no crash reporting, and no advertising SDK. We have no database of users, wallets, balances, or transactions.

Where your data lives

  • Your keys are generated on your device and never leave it unencrypted.
  • Your encrypted wallet backup is stored in your own iCloud account (iCloud Drive and iCloud Keychain), end-to-end under Apple's infrastructure. We cannot read it.
  • Your transaction history is derived on-device from the public Bitcoin blockchain.
  • Payment cards you send in iMessage travel through Apple's end-to-end encrypted Messages service like any other message.

Third parties the app talks to

  • Public Bitcoin APIs (currently mempool.space and blockstream.info) for reading the blockchain, fee estimates, price display, and broadcasting transactions. These providers can observe the addresses queried and your IP address, subject to their own privacy policies. No identity information accompanies these requests, and we never upload wallet-level identifiers (such as extended public keys).
  • Apple — iCloud storage/sync and iMessage transport, per your Apple account settings and Apple's privacy policy.

This website

The pages on this site render entirely in your browser, make no external requests, set no cookies, and run no analytics. Payment-card fallback pages parse the link's details locally to display them; gift links' secrets are never displayed, stored, or transmitted by this site.

Your responsibility

OrangeBubbles is self-custodial. Your wallet is recoverable through your own iCloud account or your recovery phrase; we cannot recover, freeze, or move funds for anyone — including you. Gift links are bearer instruments: anyone holding the link can claim the funds.
